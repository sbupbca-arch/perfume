Est. 1888 • Grasse, France

The Sacred Tradition Of Haute Parfumerie

Founded in 1888 in the historic perfume capital of Grasse, Maison d'Élixir was born out of a royal commission to distill the rare damask roses of the French Riviera into liquid gold. Over four generations of master perfumers, our house has protected secret formulas preserved in handwritten leather codices.

Every flacon of Maison d'Élixir is a harmonious symphony composed of up to 200 rare natural botanicals—hand-harvested at dawn when the oil concentration reaches its peak.

Chronicles Of Excellence

Historic Brand Milestones

1888

The Dawn in Grasse

Master Alchemist Henri de Élixir constructs the first copper alembic stills in the hills of Grasse, producing bespoke scents for European royalty.

1924

Opening at Place Vendôme, Paris

Maison d'Élixir opens its flagship salon in Paris, quickly becoming the benchmark for international high-fashion fragrance art.

1978

Royal Warrant & Private Reserves

Awarded prestigious royal seals for crafting exclusive private oud blends aged for over a decade in vintage wooden casks.

2026

The Sustainable Extrait Era

Pioneering 100% refillable crystal flacons, blockchain authenticity certificates, and zero-carbon ethical flower harvesting worldwide.

Hand Harvested
